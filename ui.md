## 1. Introduction — Who Is This For?

The **Touch of Hope CBO Portal** is the digital home for Touch of Hope Community Based Organization — supporting orphaned children, less-privileged families, and persons with disabilities across Kenya.

This manual covers **every type of user**:

| Role | Icon | Access Level | Description |
|------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Visitor** | 🌐 | No login needed | Anyone browsing the public website |
| **Member** | 👤 | Basic portal | Registered supporters — donate and track history |
| **Volunteer** | 🤝 | Member + volunteer hub | Field activity participants |
| **Secretary** | 📋 | + Volunteers, Events, Announcements | Operations management |
| **Treasurer** | 💰 | + Finances, Campaigns | Financial oversight |
| **Chairman** | 👑 | Full access | System administration |

> **Quick links:** `https://touchofhope.org` · Support: `chairman@touchofhope.org`

## 2. Public Website (No Account Needed)

**Anyone** can browse the public website without creating an account.

### What you can see

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 📊 **Live Statistics** | Total members, funds raised, active campaigns, volunteers |
| 💰 **Campaigns** | Active fundraising drives with real-time progress bars |
| 📅 **Events** | Upcoming community activities and their locations |
| 🌱 **Projects** | Active and completed community initiatives with impact summaries |
| 📢 **Announcements** | Latest news and updates from the organization |

### How to navigate

1. Go to **`https://touchofhope.org`** in any browser.
2. Use the **top navigation bar** to jump to Campaigns, Events, Projects.
3. Scroll down to see all sections on the homepage.
4. Click **"Donate / Login"** in the top-right to donate or create an account.

### Donating as a visitor (without an account)

You can donate without registering:

1. Click **Donate / Login** → you will be taken to the login/donate page.
2. Optionally log in, or continue to the donate page.
3. Select a campaign (or leave unselected for the General Fund).
4. Enter your amount and choose a payment method.
5. A **receipt will be emailed** to the address you provide.

## 3. Creating an Account

### Step-by-step registration

1. Go to the website → click **Donate / Login** → click the **Register** tab.
2. Fill in:
   - **Full Name** *(required)*
   - **Email address** *(required — used for login and receipts)*
   - **Phone number** *(required)*
   - **Password** *(required — see requirements below)*
   - **National ID** *(optional)*
3. Click **Submit Application**.
4. **Check your email** — you will receive a verification link within 2 minutes.
5. Click the link in the email to verify your address.
6. **Wait for the Chairperson to approve** your account — you will receive an email when activated.
7. Return to the login page and sign in.

> ⚠️ **Check your Spam/Junk folder** if you do not receive the verification email.

### Password requirements

Your password must contain:

- ✅ Minimum **8 characters**
- ✅ At least one **UPPERCASE letter** (A–Z)
- ✅ At least one **number** (0–9)
- ❌ Do not use your name or email as your password
- ❌ Never share your password with anyone

**Good password examples:** `Kenya@2025` · `TOH#Member1` · `Hope4All!`

## 4. Signing In & Password Reset

### Signing in

1. Go to **`https://touchofhope.org/login.html`**
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In →**
4. You will be taken to your personal portal dashboard.

> ℹ️ Your session stays active for **8 hours**. After that you will need to sign in again.

### Forgot your password?

1. Go to the login page.
2. Click **"Forgot password?"** below the Sign In button.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your email for a **password reset link** (valid for **1 hour**).
5. Click the link → set a new password.
6. Return to the login page and sign in.

> ⚠️ If the reset link has expired, repeat the forgot-password process to get a new one.

### Signing out

Click **Sign Out** in the top-right corner of the portal at any time.

## 6. Payment Methods

### 📱 M-Pesa (Recommended for Kenya)

**Best for:** Kenyan users with M-Pesa on their phone.

1. Select the **M-Pesa** tab on the donation page.
2. Enter your **name** and your **M-Pesa phone number** (e.g. `0712345678` or `+254712345678`).
3. Click **💚 Pay Now via M-Pesa**.
4. An **STK Push pop-up will appear on your phone**.
5. Enter your **M-Pesa PIN** on your phone to authorize.
6. Payment status updates automatically on the portal within 30 seconds.

> ⚠️ You have **60 seconds** to enter your PIN after the prompt appears. If it expires, click Pay Now again.

---

### 💳 Card (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay)

**Best for:** International donors or those with a debit/credit card.

1. Select the **Card** tab.
2. Enter your **name** and **email** (for the receipt).
3. Enter your card details in the secure Stripe form.
4. On **iPhone/Mac using Safari** → Apple Pay appears automatically.
5. On **Android using Chrome** → Google Pay appears automatically.
6. Click **💳 Pay with Card**.

> 🔒 Payments are processed by **Stripe** — Touch of Hope never stores your card details.

---

### 🅿️ PayPal

**Best for:** International donors with a PayPal account; also accepts crypto via PayPal.

1. Select the **PayPal** tab.
2. Enter your donation amount (automatically converted to USD).
3. Click **🅿️ Pay with PayPal**.
4. A **new window opens** with the PayPal checkout.
5. Log in to PayPal (or use guest checkout with card).
6. Approve the payment → return to the portal.

> ℹ️ PayPal also supports **cryptocurrency** payments (Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.) through PayPal Crypto inside the PayPal checkout window.

---

### 🏦 Bank Transfer

**Best for:** Large organizational donations.

1. Select the **Bank** tab.
2. Note the bank details shown:
   - **Bank:** Equity Bank Kenya
   - **Account Name:** Touch of Hope CBO
   - **Account No:** shown on screen
   - **Branch:** Nairobi CBD
3. Make the transfer from your bank or mobile banking app.
4. **Email your proof of payment** to `treasurer@touchofhope.org`.
5. The treasurer will record and confirm your donation.

## 7. Volunteer Portal

> 🤝 Available after your volunteer application is **approved by the Secretary**.

### Applying to become a volunteer

1. Log in → go to **My Volunteer Hub** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Volunteer Application** panel at the bottom.
3. Enter your **skills** (comma-separated, e.g. `Teaching, Medical, IT`).
4. Select your **availability** (Weekdays / Weekends / Full-time / Flexible).
5. Write a short **bio** about yourself.
6. Click **Submit Application**.
7. The Secretary will review it. You'll be emailed when approved.

### After approval — using the Volunteer Hub

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|--------------|
| **My Events** | Events you are already registered for (date, location) |
| **Available Events** | Upcoming events you can join — click **Join →** to register |

> You can update your skills and availability at any time by resubmitting the application form.

## 8. Secretary Portal

> 📋 Available to users with the Secretary role or higher.

### Managing Volunteers

1. Click **Volunteers** in the sidebar.
2. Volunteers with a yellow **"Pending"** badge are awaiting approval.
3. Click **Approve** → the volunteer receives an email and gains Volunteer portal access.

### Creating Events

1. Click **Events** → **+ Create Event**.
2. Fill in: title, date, location, number of volunteers needed, description.
3. Click **Create Event** — appears on the public website and in the Volunteer Hub immediately.
4. To delete: click the red **✕** button on any event card.

> ℹ️ Events with a past date can still be kept for historical records — just change their status to "Completed."

### Posting Announcements

1. Click **Announcements** → **+ Post Announcement**.
2. Enter a **title** and the **content**.
3. Check **"Visible on public website"** for public announcements.
4. Uncheck it for internal-only notices (visible in portal only).
5. Click **Post**.
6. To delete: click the red **✕** on any announcement card.

## 9. Treasurer Portal

> 💰 Available to users with the Treasurer role or higher.

### Recording a Manual Donation (Cash or Bank)

When someone donates outside the online system:

1. Click **Finances** → **+ Record Donation**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Donor Name** *(required)*
   - **Donor Email** *(optional — triggers automatic receipt)*
   - **Amount** *(required)*
   - **Method** — Cash or Bank
   - **Campaign** *(leave blank for General Fund)*
   - **Reference** — bank transaction ID or M-Pesa receipt number
   - **Date** received
   - **Notes** *(optional)*
3. Click **Record** — the donation is saved as "completed" and the campaign progress updates instantly.

> ✅ If an email was provided, a receipt is sent automatically.

### Financial Summary

The **Finances** page shows:

| Card | Information |
|------|------------|
| Total Raised | Sum of all completed donations |
| Average Donation | Mean donation value |
| M-Pesa Count | Number of M-Pesa transactions |
| Stripe Total | Total collected via card payments |

Below the cards: a full paginated table of all donations with donor, campaign, amount, method, status, and date.

## 10. Chairman Portal

> 👑 Full system access. All features of all roles, plus:

### Approving User Accounts

1. Click **Users** in the sidebar.
2. Pending accounts show a yellow **"Pending"** badge.
3. Click **Approve** → the user receives a welcome email and can log in.
4. To change a user's role: use the **role dropdown** next to their name.
5. To suspend a user: click **Suspend** (account deactivated but not deleted).

> ⚠️ Always change the default Chairman password (`Chairman@2025`) immediately after first login.

### Creating Campaigns

1. Click **Campaigns** → **+ New Campaign**.
2. Enter: title, description, target amount (KSh), optional end date.
3. Click **Launch Campaign** — appears on the public website immediately.
4. Campaign progress updates automatically as donations are received.
5. To delete: click the red **✕** on the campaign card.

### Creating Projects

1. Click **Projects** → **+ New Project**.
2. Enter: title, location, start date, status, description, impact summary.
3. The **impact summary** (e.g. `60 children monthly`) is displayed prominently on the public site.
4. Click **Save Project**.

Click **Finances** → scroll to see the audit log section (Chairman-only). Shows all admin actions: logins, approvals, role changes, donations recorded, backups, restores.

## 11. Backup & Restore

> 🗃️ Chairman only. Protects all data — members, donations, campaigns, events, and more.

### Setting Up Automatic Backups

1. Click **Backup & Restore** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Frequency**: Daily *(recommended)*, Weekly, Hourly, or Monthly.
3. Set **Backup Time**: `02:00` recommended (low-traffic hours).
4. Set **Retain backups for**: `30` days (older backups are deleted automatically).
5. Toggle **💾 Local Backup** ON — saves copies on the server.
6. Toggle **☁️ Cloud Backup** ON *(optional)* — select provider (GCS / AWS S3 / Azure) and enter bucket name.
7. Click **Save Configuration**.

The system now runs backups automatically. ✅

### Running a Manual Backup

1. Go to **Backup & Restore**.
2. Click **▶ Run Backup Now**.
3. Wait for the green confirmation toast.
4. The backup appears in the **Backup History** list.

### Restoring from a Backup

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** Restoring **replaces all current data** with the selected backup snapshot. A safety snapshot is created automatically before every restore. This cannot be undone.

1. Go to **Backup & Restore** → scroll to **Restore Database**.
2. Use the **Select Backup to Restore** dropdown — choose a backup by filename and date.
3. Click **♻️ Restore Selected Backup**.
4. Read the warning and click **OK** to confirm.
5. **Do not close your browser** — wait 1–3 minutes.
6. A success message confirms the restore is complete.

## 12. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| ❓ Didn't receive verification email | Check Spam/Junk folder. Wait 5 minutes. Contact `chairman@touchofhope.org`. |
| ❓ Account says "Pending Approval" | Email verified — awaiting Chairperson approval. You'll be emailed when done. |
| ❓ Forgot password | Login page → "Forgot password?" → enter email → check inbox for reset link. |
| ❓ M-Pesa STK Push didn't appear | Ensure phone number matches your M-Pesa-registered number. Check network signal. Try again. |
| ❓ M-Pesa shows "Payment failed" | Check M-Pesa balance. Try again or use a different method. |
| ❓ Page shows demo/test data | Backend server is offline. Contact the system administrator. |
| ❓ "Insufficient permissions" error | Your role doesn't have access. Contact the Chairman to update your role. |
| ❓ Backup shows "failed" | Contact the developer — `mongodump` may not be installed, or cloud credentials may be wrong. |
| ❓ PayPal window shows an error | Try a different browser. Ensure your PayPal account has sufficient funds. |
| ❓ Accidentally suspended a user | Go to Users → use the role dropdown next to the user → select any role to reactivate. |
| ❓ Campaign progress not updating | Progress updates automatically when a donation is confirmed. Pending M-Pesa payments update within 30 seconds. |
| ❓ Reset link expired | Request a new one using the forgot-password form. Links expire after 1 hour. |
