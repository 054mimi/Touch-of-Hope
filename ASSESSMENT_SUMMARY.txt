## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Test locally first** — Don't skip this! Find issues before production
2. **Use payment test credentials** — Stripe test card: `4242 4242 4242 4242`
3. **Keep .env secure** — Never commit, always use .gitignore
4. **Monitor logs** — Winston will save errors to files
5. **Test backup/restore** — Do this monthly, especially before launch
6. **Set up monitoring** — Sentry, DataDog, or New Relic (catch errors early)
